{{怎样开始||How to start}}

{{无论哪条业务线，合作都从同样的四步开始。||Whichever practice you need, an engagement begins with the same four steps.}}

  1. {{写信||Write to us}}

    {{说明你现在的情况和想解决的问题，不需要准备正式材料。||Tell us where you are and what you want to solve. No formal materials are needed.}}

  2. {{一次沟通||A first conversation}}

    {{我们把问题问清楚，也告诉你哪些事我们做不了。||We make sure we understand the problem, and we tell you what we cannot do.}}

  3. {{书面方案||A written proposal}}

    {{范围、周期、交付物和费用写在纸面上，由你确认。||Scope, timeline, deliverables and fees are set down in writing for you to confirm.}}

  4. {{开始交付||Delivery begins}}

    {{短周期推进，让你尽早看到可以用的东西。||We work in short cycles so that you see something usable early.}}
