02{{可靠比新奇重要||Dependable matters more than novel}}

{{企业买的是结果，不是模型。一个系统值不值得，不看它用了多新的技术，而看它在一个普通的星期二下午是否照常工作。||A business buys an outcome, not a model. Whether a system is worth having depends not on how new its technology is, but on whether it works as usual on an ordinary Tuesday afternoon.}}

{{对每天要用它的人来说，可靠有三层意思。||For the people who use it every day, dependable means three things.}}

  • {{答案可以核对。系统给出回答的同时，指出依据在哪里。使用者不需要相信它，只需要能检查它。||Answers can be checked. The system gives its answer and shows where it came from. Users do not have to trust it. They only have to be able to check it.}}
  • {{表现不会漂移。同样的问题，今天和下个月得到质量相当的回答。每次改动之后，都用同一组问题重新检验。||Behavior does not drift. The same question gets an answer of the same quality today and next month. After every change, the same set of questions is run again.}}
  • {{出了问题有人可找。有一个具体的人对系统负责，使用者知道是谁。||There is someone to call. A named person is responsible for the system, and users know who it is.}}

{{新技术当然重要。但我们的顺序是：先用经过验证的方案把事情做成，再在不影响稳定的地方尝试新的。反过来做的项目，演示时很精彩，之后却没有人用。||New technology matters, of course. But our order is this: first get the job done with proven approaches, then try new ones where stability is not at risk. Projects done the other way round make a fine demo and are then left unused.}}

03{{门槛决定谁能受益||The barrier decides who benefits}}

{{同样的技术，大公司用得上，小公司用不上，原因很少出在技术本身。挡在中小企业面前的是三道门槛。||The same technology reaches large companies and misses small ones, and the reason is rarely the technology. Three barriers stand in front of smaller businesses.}}

{{成本。不只是硬件的价格，还有试错的代价。大公司买错一次可以重来，小公司往往不能。所以我们主张从小处开始，先测量，再投入。||Cost. Not only the price of hardware, but the price of getting it wrong. A large company can buy the wrong thing and start again. A small one often cannot. So we argue for starting small and measuring before investing.}}

{{专业知识。小公司没有人专门研究这些。一个方案如果需要一支团队才能维持，对小公司来说就等于不存在。方案必须简单到现有的人接得住。||Expertise. A small company has nobody whose job is to study this. An approach that needs a team to keep it running might as well not exist. It has to be simple enough for the people already there to take over.}}

{{信任。把客户资料和内部文件交给一个新系统，需要理由。这个理由不该是供应商的保证，而是可以检查的安排：数据在哪里，谁能看到，留下了什么记录。我们的做法写在负责任交付政策里。||Trust. Handing customer records and internal documents to a new system needs a reason. That reason should not be a supplier's assurance. It should be arrangements that can be inspected: where the data is, who can see it, what record is kept. Ours are set out in the responsible delivery policy.}}

{{降低这三道门槛，是我们工作的主要内容。||Lowering these three barriers is most of what we do.}}

04{{验证先于投入||Validation comes before investment}}

{{知道一个想法行不通，最便宜的时刻是在动手之前。这句话人人同意，却很少有人照做，因为动手做比找人问更让人安心。||The cheapest moment to learn that an idea does not work is before you build it. Everyone agrees and few act on it, because building feels safer than asking.}}

{{AI 让验证变得又快又省：访谈可以很快整理，方案可以很快起草，样品可以很快做出来。它也带来一个新的陷阱：用模型的回答代替真实客户的反应。模型的赞同不是需求。||AI makes validation fast and cheap: interviews are quickly sorted, offers quickly drafted, mocks quickly made. It also sets a new trap, which is to take a model's answer for a real customer's reaction. A model's approval is not demand.}}

{{所以我们要求每一次验证都落到真人身上，并且在看到结果之前写下通过的标准。结果不好的时候照实说，是这项工作里最有价值的部分。||So we require every validation to end with real people, and the pass mark to be written before any result is seen. Saying so plainly when the result is poor is the most valuable part of the work.}}

05{{人在中心||People at the center}}

{{我们的口号是「以人为核，智能环绕」。它不是修辞，而是一条设计规则：AI 提出建议，人来做决定。||Our slogan is "People at the core. Intelligence in orbit." It is not a figure of speech. It is a design rule: AI proposes, and people decide.}}

{{落到系统里，这意味着三件事。重要的输出在生效之前有人复核。系统没有把握的时候明说。使用者随时可以绕过它，用原来的办法把事情做完。||Inside a system this means three things. Outputs that matter are checked by a person before they take effect. The system says so when it is unsure. And users can always go around it and finish the job the old way.}}

{{这也是我们衡量成败的方式。一个系统让使用它的人更有能力，它就成功了。一个系统部署完成、验收通过，却没有人愿意用，它就失败了，不管合同上怎么写。||It is also how we measure success. A system that makes its users more capable has succeeded. A system that is deployed and accepted but that nobody chooses to use has failed, whatever the contract says.}}

06{{我们不确定的事||What we are unsure about}}

  • {{算力成本会降得多快。这直接影响「买还是租」的答案，我们的建议会随之调整。||How fast the cost of computing will fall. This bears directly on whether to buy or rent, and our advice will move with it.}}
  • {{哪些工作对 AI 来说会长期困难。今天需要人复核的环节，将来哪些可以放手，我们还说不准。||Which tasks will stay hard for AI. We cannot yet say which of today's human checks can one day be dropped.}}
  • {{小团队怎样在速度和谨慎之间取舍。大公司的流程搬到三个人的团队里行不通，完全没有流程也不行。合适的分寸在哪里，我们还在摸索。||How small teams should balance speed and care. A large company's process does not fit a team of three, and no process at all does not work either. We are still finding the right measure.}}
