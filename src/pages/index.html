{{企业级 AI 基础设施||Enterprise-Grade AI Infrastructure}}

{{在你自己的环境里部署模型与推理，接入业务系统，上线后持续看护。||Models and inference deployed in your own environment, connected to your systems, looked after once live.}}

{{中小企业普惠算力||Accessible Computing for SMBs}}

{{先量真实用量，再决定买、租还是共用。不为用不上的算力付钱。||Measure real use first, then buy, rent or share. Never pay for capacity you will not use.}}

{{AI 商业验证||AI-Powered Business Validation}}

{{在投入重金之前，用 AI 检验需求是否成立、模式是否走得通。||Before serious money goes in, use AI to test whether the demand is real and the model works.}}

{{早期创业融资对接||Venture Capital Access for Early-Stage Startups}}

{{为 A 轮前的团队做好融资准备，在合适的时候对接投资人。||Get a pre-Series A team ready to raise, and make introductions when the fit is right.}}

{{赋能独立创业者||Empowering Independent Entrepreneurs}}

{{把 AI、算力与资本对接放在一起，让一个人也能撑起一门生意。||AI, computing and access to capital together, so one person can carry a business.}}

{{我们的看法||What we believe}}

{{企业买的是结果，不是模型。成本、专业知识和信任，是挡在中小企业面前的三道门槛。知道一个想法行不通，最便宜的时刻是在动手之前。AI 提出建议，人来做决定。||A business buys an outcome, not a model. Cost, expertise and trust are the three barriers in front of smaller businesses. The cheapest moment to learn an idea is wrong is before you build it. AI proposes. People decide.}}
