{{此前，我们按服务类型介绍自己：部署、售后、顾问。客户要自己判断哪一项与自己有关。现在改为按服务对象来分，先找到「我是谁」，再看「能得到什么」。||Until now we described ourselves by type of service: deployment, support, advisory. Clients had to work out for themselves which one applied to them. We now group the work by who it serves, so that you find yourself first and the offer second.}}

{{为企业||For businesses}}

  • {{企业级 AI 基础设施：为模型部署、推理和业务应用提供可靠、可扩展的 AI 基础设施。||Enterprise-Grade AI Infrastructure: reliable, scalable AI infrastructure for model deployment, inference, and business applications.}}
  • {{中小企业普惠算力：灵活、划算的算力方案，让中小企业也能用上 AI、放手创新。||Accessible Computing for SMBs: flexible, cost-effective computing solutions that make AI adoption and innovation accessible to small and medium-sized businesses.}}
  • {{AI 商业验证：用 AI 做需求验证、商业模式测试和流程自动化，缩短从想法到市场的路。||AI-Powered Business Validation: AI-powered demand validation, business model testing, and workflow automation to accelerate the journey from idea to market.}}

{{为创业者||For founders}}

  • {{早期创业融资对接：为 A 轮前的创业团队对接投资人与创投资源，帮助做好融资准备、支持早期成长。||Venture Capital Access for Early-Stage Startups: investor connections and venture resources for pre-Series A startups, supporting fundraising readiness and early-stage growth.}}
  • {{赋能独立创业者：把 AI、算力与资本对接放在一起，帮助创业者和独立专业人士把专长与想法变成可持续的事业。||Empowering Independent Entrepreneurs: bringing together AI, computing, and capital access to help founders and independent professionals turn expertise and ideas into sustainable businesses.}}

{{五条业务线共用同一套能力：AI、算力，以及把事情做成的经验。完整介绍见业务总览。||The five practices share one set of capabilities: AI, computing, and the experience of getting things done. The full picture is on the practices page.}}
