{{我们的做法||Our approach}}

{{最小够用||Only what is needed}}

{{只索取工作所需的数据，只开放完成工作所需的权限。||We ask only for the data the work needs, and open only the access the work requires.}}

{{数据不出环境||Data stays in place}}

{{敏感数据可以留在客户自己的环境里处理。本地部署就是为此而设。||Sensitive data can be processed inside the client's own environment. On-premises deployment exists for this.}}

{{留痕可查||Everything leaves a trace}}

{{谁在什么时候用了什么，有记录可查。日志本身也受权限保护。||There is a record of who used what, and when. The logs themselves are access-controlled.}}

{{人工复核||A person checks}}

{{涉及敏感数据的输出，在生效之前由人复核。||Outputs that involve sensitive data are checked by a person before they take effect.}}

{{报告安全问题||Report a security issue}}

{{如果你发现本网站或我们交付的系统存在安全问题，请告诉我们。||If you find a security problem in this website or in a system we delivered, please tell us.}}

>{{写信报告||Report by email}}

  1. {{写信给我们||Write to us}}

    {{发到 sales@cv-etron.com，邮件标题写「安全问题」。||Send it to sales@cv-etron.com with the subject line "Security issue".}}

  2. {{说明细节||Include the details}}

    {{问题出在哪里、怎样重现，以及你认为可能造成的影响。||Where the problem is, how to reproduce it, and what you think its impact could be.}}

  3. {{我们确认并调查||We acknowledge and investigate}}

    {{我们会回信确认收到，进行调查，并把结论告诉你。||We write back to confirm receipt, investigate, and tell you what we found.}}

  4. {{修复之后再公开||Disclose after the fix}}

    {{在我们有机会修复之前，请不要公开细节。||Please do not make the details public before we have had a chance to fix the problem.}}
