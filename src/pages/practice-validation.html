{{我们做什么||What we do}}

{{需求验证||Demand validation}}

{{把最关键的假设写出来，用最省的办法去检验：访谈整理、方案对比、可点击的样品。||We write down the riskiest assumption and test it the cheapest way: interview synthesis, side-by-side offers, a clickable mock.}}

{{商业模式测试||Business model testing}}

{{在投入之前算清楚钱从哪里来、成本在哪里，并让真实客户对报价作出反应。||Before you invest, we work out where the money comes from and where the costs sit, and put a real offer in front of real customers.}}

{{流程自动化||Workflow automation}}

{{找出重复、规则清楚的工作，交给 AI 处理，并保留人工复核。||We find work that is repetitive and rule-bound, hand it to AI, and keep a person checking the results.}}

{{高管顾问||Executive advisory}}

{{为企业主和高管提供一对一的 AI 顾问会谈，也可以是较长期的顾问合作。||One-to-one AI advisory sessions for owners and executives, or a longer advisory engagement.}}

{{我们怎么做||How we work on it}}

{{测试开始之前，我们和你约定什么结果算通过。AI 能让验证更快，但它替代不了真实客户的付款和承诺，所以每一轮测试都要落到真人身上。结果不好，我们照实说。合作中的做事方式见服务准则。||Before a test begins, we agree with you what result counts as a pass. AI makes validation faster, but it cannot stand in for real customers paying or committing, so every round ends with real people. When the result is poor, we say so. How we conduct an engagement is set out in the service charter.}}
