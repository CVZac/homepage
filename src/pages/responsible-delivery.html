01{{为什么有这份政策||Why this policy exists}}

{{企业里的 AI 系统接触的是真实的数据，影响的是真实的决定。数据越敏感，系统上线之前需要的保护就越强。||An AI system inside a business touches real data and affects real decisions. The more sensitive the data, the stronger the safeguards it needs before it goes live.}}

{{这份政策写下我们对自己的要求。它是自愿的：没有人要求我们这样做，我们选择公开它，是为了让客户可以对照检查。||This policy sets out what we require of ourselves. It is voluntary. Nobody obliges us to follow it, and we publish it so that clients can hold us to it.}}

02{{部署等级||Deployment levels}}

{{每一次部署，都按系统会接触到的最敏感的数据归入四个等级之一。||Every deployment is placed in one of four levels, according to the most sensitive data the system will touch.}}

{{等级||Level}}{{数据||Data}}{{运行位置||Where it may run}}{{访问与日志||Access and logging}}{{人工复核||Human review}}
D1{{公开信息，例如已发布的产品资料||Public information, such as published product material}}{{任何合适的环境||Any suitable environment}}{{基本的账号管理||Basic account management}}{{对外发布前复核||Before anything is published}}
D2{{内部资料，例如制度、流程、会议记录||Internal material, such as policies, procedures and meeting notes}}{{经客户认可的环境||An environment the client has approved}}{{按角色授权，保留使用记录||Access by role, with a record of use}}{{用于决策前复核||Before use in a decision}}
D3{{客户信息与个人信息||Customer and personal information}}{{客户自己的环境，或客户指定的专有环境||The client's own environment, or a private one the client designates}}{{最小权限，完整日志，定期查看||Least privilege, full logs, reviewed regularly}}{{涉及个人的输出逐项复核||Every output that concerns an individual}}
D4{{受监管或高度敏感的数据||Regulated or highly sensitive data}}{{仅限客户自己的环境||Inside the client's own environment only}}{{最小权限，完整日志，由客户指定专人审查||Least privilege, full logs, audited by a person the client names}}{{所有输出在生效前复核||Every output, before it takes effect}}

{{拿不准属于哪一级时，按更高的一级处理。||When it is unclear which level applies, we use the higher one.}}

03{{上线前的检查||Checks before launch}}

  1. {{给数据分类||Classify the data}}

    {{列出系统会接触的每一类数据，并标明敏感程度。||List every kind of data the system will touch and mark its sensitivity.}}

  2. {{确定等级||Choose the level}}

    {{按其中最敏感的一类确定部署等级，并与客户确认。||Set the deployment level by the most sensitive kind, and confirm it with the client.}}

  3. {{落实保护措施||Put the safeguards in place}}

    {{按该等级的要求配置运行位置、权限、日志和复核方式。||Configure location, access, logging and review as the level requires.}}

  4. {{用真实问题测试||Test with real questions}}

    {{使用客户提供的真实问题和期望答案进行测试，结果留档。||Test with real questions and expected answers supplied by the client, and keep the results.}}

  5. {{客户确认||The client signs off}}

    {{客户查看测试结果和保护措施，书面确认。||The client reviews the test results and safeguards and confirms in writing.}}

  6. {{上线||Launch}}

    {{前五步完成之后，系统才上线。||The system goes live only after the first five steps are complete.}}

{{每一步的具体检查项，见上线前的检查清单。||The detailed items for each step are in our pre-launch checklist.}}

04{{出现问题时||When something goes wrong}}

  • {{及时告知。发现问题后，我们主动通知客户，说明已知的情况和尚不清楚的部分。||Tell the client promptly. When we find a problem we notify the client ourselves, saying what is known and what is not yet clear.}}
  • {{先控制影响。必要时停用受影响的功能，退回上一个可用的版本。||Contain it first. Where necessary we switch off the affected function and roll back to the last working version.}}
  • {{修复并验证。修复之后，重新运行测试，确认问题不再出现。||Fix and verify. After the fix, the tests are run again to confirm the problem does not recur.}}
  • {{写下经过。记录发生了什么、原因是什么、改了什么，并交给客户。||Write it down. We record what happened, why, and what was changed, and give the record to the client.}}
