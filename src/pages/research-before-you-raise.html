{{一句话说清问题||The problem in one sentence}}

{{谁，在什么情况下，遇到什么麻烦。说不成一句话，通常是因为还没想清楚，而不是因为问题太复杂。||Who, in what situation, runs into what trouble. When it will not fit in one sentence, the usual reason is that it has not been thought through, not that it is too complex.}}

{{需求的证据和来历||Evidence of demand, and its source}}

{{有多少人用、多少人付费，当然重要。更重要的是这些数字是怎么来的，因为投资人一定会追问来源。朋友捧场得来的数字，和陌生人主动找来的数字，分量不同。照实说。||How many people use the product and how many pay matters, of course. Where those numbers came from matters more, because investors will ask. Numbers from friends doing you a favor weigh differently from numbers from strangers who found you. Say which is which.}}

{{一个简单的赚钱模型||A simple model of how money is made}}

{{一位客户带来多少收入，获得他要花多少钱，服务他要花多少钱。不需要复杂的表格。需要的是每个数字都说得出依据，并且知道哪一个最没把握。||What one customer brings in, what it costs to win that customer, and what it costs to serve them. No elaborate spreadsheet is needed. What is needed is a reason behind every number, and knowing which number you trust least.}}

{{这轮钱要证明什么||What the round should prove}}

{{「用于研发和市场」不是答案。答案应该是：这笔钱花完的时候，我们会知道哪件今天还不知道的事。||"Research and marketing" is not an answer. The answer is what you will know when the money is spent that you do not know today.}}

{{可以直接给人看的文件||Documents you can share}}

{{股权结构、主要合同、知识产权的归属。这些文件临时整理最容易出错，也最容易让一场本来顺利的谈话停下来。提前整理好，放在一个随时可以分享的地方。||The ownership structure, the main contracts, and who owns the intellectual property. Assembled in a hurry, these are where mistakes happen, and where a conversation that was going well comes to a halt. Put them in order early and keep them somewhere you can share at any time.}}

{{准备做不到的事||What preparation cannot do}}

{{准备让谈话更有效率，也让你更早知道自己差在哪里。它不能保证融到钱。有些团队准备充分却没有融到，原因可能与他们无关。||Preparation makes conversations more efficient and shows you sooner where you fall short. It cannot guarantee funding. Some well-prepared teams do not raise, for reasons that may have nothing to do with them.}}

{{本文是一般性的准备建议，不构成投资建议。任何准备都不能保证融资成功。||This article is general guidance on preparation. It is not investment advice, and no preparation guarantees a successful raise.}}
