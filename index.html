    07常见问题FAQ

    关于费用，你可能想问Questions about cost

    没找到答案？直接发邮件给我们。Can't find your answer? Just email us.

    为什么硬件费用不包含在套餐里？Why isn't hardware included in the plans?

    硬件价格随市场波动大，不同需求的配置差异也很大。把服务费和硬件费分开，你能清楚看到每一分钱花在哪里。你可以自行采购，也可以让我们按渠道价代采，只收 5% 服务费并提供原厂报价单。Hardware prices move with the market and vary widely by need. Keeping service and hardware separate shows you exactly where every yuan goes. Buy it yourself, or let us source it at channel price for a 5% fee, with the original vendor quote.

    计算器算出来的价格准吗？How accurate is the estimator?

    标准需求下，计算器的结果就是最终服务价格。如果有定制需求，我们会在正式报价单中逐项列出，签约前与你确认。For standard needs, the estimate is the final service price. Anything custom is itemized in the formal quote and confirmed with you before signing.

    可以分期付款吗？Can I pay in installments?

    部署项目默认分三期支付（50% / 40% / 10%）。售后可以月付；顾问的季度和年度方案也可以按月分期，在邮件中说明即可。Deployments are paid in three stages (50% / 40% / 10%). Support can be paid monthly, and quarterly or annual advisory can be split into monthly payments on request.

    部署自带的质保期和全天候售后有什么区别？What's the difference between the warranty and 24/7 Support?

    质保期内，我们免费修复部署相关的问题，工作日远程处理。全天候售后则提供 7×24 监控、承诺响应时间、定期更新和现场支持，适合对稳定性要求高的业务。During the warranty we fix deployment-related issues for free, remotely on business days. 24/7 Support adds round-the-clock monitoring, guaranteed response times, regular updates and on-site help for business-critical systems.

    我们的数据会离开公司吗？Will our data leave the company?

    不会。私有化部署意味着模型和数据都运行在你自己的服务器上。远程运维必须经过你的授权，并且随时可以关闭。No. Private deployment means models and data run on your own servers. Remote maintenance requires your authorization and can be switched off at any time.

    不满意可以退款吗？What if I'm not satisfied?

    首次顾问会谈不满意，全额退款。部署项目在开工前取消，全额退还定金；开工后按已完成的工作量结算。If your first advisory session isn't useful, you get a full refund. Cancel a deployment before work starts and your deposit is returned in full; after that, you pay only for work completed.

    外地或海外可以服务吗？Do you work outside your city, or overseas?

    可以。远程服务不受地域限制；外地现场服务的差旅费按实际发生报销，出发前与你确认预算。Yes. Remote services work anywhere. On-site travel is billed at cost, with the budget agreed before we go.

    什么情况下需要企业定制版？When do I need Enterprise?

    超过 500 人、多地点部署、需要国产芯片适配或定制开发，或者需要集团级顾问服务时。发邮件说明规模和需求，我们会在 2 个工作日内给出定制方案。Over 500 users, multiple sites, domestic chips, custom development, or group-level advisory. Email us your scale and needs and we'll send a tailored proposal within 2 business days.
