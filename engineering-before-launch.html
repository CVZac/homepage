AI 系统上线前的一张检查清单A checklist before an AI system goes live

系统能跑通演示，和系统可以交给每天要用它的人，是两回事。中间差的就是这张清单。A system that gets through a demo and a system you can hand to people who will rely on it every day are different things. This checklist is the difference.

本文内容On this page
  1. 数据Data
  2. 回答质量Answer quality
  3. 失败的时候When it fails
  4. 权限与日志Access and logs
  5. 交接Handover

数据Data

  • 进入系统的每一类数据都列了出来，并标明敏感程度。Every kind of data entering the system is listed, with its sensitivity marked.
  • 每一类数据谁可以看到，已经写下来并配置好。Who may see each kind of data is written down and configured.
  • 测试时用过的真实数据，知道存在哪里、什么时候删除。For real data used in testing, it is known where it is stored and when it will be deleted.

回答质量Answer quality

  • 有一组固定的真实问题和期望答案，由实际使用的人提供。There is a fixed set of real questions with expected answers, supplied by the people who will use the system.
  • 每次改动之后都重跑这组问题，结果有记录。The set is re-run after every change, and the results are recorded.
  • 回答能指出依据的来源，使用者可以自己核对。Answers point to their sources, so users can check them.
  • 已经和使用者约定好：哪些问题系统不该回答。It has been agreed with users which questions the system should not answer.

失败的时候When it fails

  • 系统没有把握时会明说，而不是编一个答案。When the system is unsure it says so, instead of inventing an answer.
  • 系统不可用时，使用者知道该怎么办、找谁。When the system is down, users know what to do and whom to ask.
  • 重要的输出在生效之前有人复核。Outputs that matter are checked by a person before they take effect.

权限与日志Access and logs

  • 每个人只能访问自己该访问的内容。Each person can reach only what they should.
  • 谁在什么时候问了什么、得到了什么，有记录可查。There is a record of who asked what, when, and what came back.
  • 日志本身的访问权限也设置好了。Access to the logs themselves is restricted too.

交接Handover

  • 上线之后由谁负责这个系统，名字已经写下来。The person responsible for the system after launch is named in writing.
  • 出问题时怎样退回上一个版本，已经演练过一次。Rolling back to the previous version has been rehearsed once.
  • 使用者接受过培训，知道它擅长什么、不擅长什么。Users have been trained and know what the system is good at and what it is not.

清单上没有一项是难事。难的是在赶进度的时候，仍然一项一项去确认。Nothing on the list is hard. What is hard is confirming each item, one by one, when the schedule is pressing.
