我们看重什么，如何行事What we value and how we act

这些原则也是我们彼此共事的方式。These principles are also how we work with one another.

以人为核People at the core

技术围着人转，而不是相反。每个方案都从使用它的人出发。Technology revolves around people, not the reverse. Every design starts from the person who will use it.

说清楚，再动手Say it clearly, then start

范围、周期和交付物在开始前写明白，过程中的变化及时说明。Scope, timeline and deliverables are written down before work begins, and changes along the way are explained promptly.

小步快跑Small steps, quickly

短周期交付，让真实用户尽早用上，再根据反馈迭代。We deliver in short cycles, get real users on the system early, and iterate on what they tell us.

用 AI 的方式做 AIBuild AI the AI-native way

我们自己的流程就建立在 AI 之上，所以知道它在哪里有用、在哪里还不行。Our own workflows run on AI, so we know where it helps and where it still falls short.

对结果负责Own the outcome

交付不是终点。系统真正被用起来，才算完成。Delivery is not the finish line. The work is done when the system is truly in use.

不夸大No overclaiming

做不到的事直接说做不到，不承诺无法保证的结果。When something cannot be done we say so, and we do not promise results we cannot guarantee.

我们怎样工作How we work

三件事说明了我们日常的工作方式。Three things describe how we work day to day.

小团队，直接面对客户Small teams, close to the client

做事的人直接和客户说话，中间没有传话的人。你会清楚自己的工作对谁有用、有没有用。The people doing the work talk to the client directly, with nobody relaying messages in between. You will know whom your work is for and whether it helped.

AI 原生的工具与流程AI-native tools and process

我们在自己的工作里全程使用 AI，并且认真对待它出错的地方。交给客户的内容，由人复核。We use AI throughout our own work, and we take its mistakes seriously. Anything that goes to a client is reviewed by a person.

把事情写下来We write things down

范围、决定和理由都落在纸面上。这让小团队不必靠开会来对齐，也让后来的人接得上。Scope, decisions and reasons are put in writing. It lets a small team stay aligned without meetings, and lets whoever comes next pick up the thread.

如何加入How to join

我们没有申请表。一封信就够了。There is no application form. A letter is enough.

目前有公开职位吗？Are there open roles?

我们不在网站上列出固定职位。每一封来信我们都会读。We do not list fixed openings on this site. We read every letter.

信里写什么？What should the letter include?

你是谁，做过什么，对哪条业务线感兴趣。能让我们看到你做过的东西最好。Who you are, what you have built, and which practice interests you. Best of all, something you made that we can look at.

之后会怎样？What happens next?

如果合适，我们会回信约一次谈话。If there is a fit, we will write back to arrange a conversation.
