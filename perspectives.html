观点Perspectives

我们对 AI 应该怎样进入企业和创业的公开立场。写下来，是为了让客户知道我们会怎么判断。Our public positions on how AI should enter businesses and start-ups. We write them down so clients know how we will judge.

我们的主张What we hold

可靠先于新奇Dependable before novel

一个系统值不值得，看它在普通的一天里是否照常工作。A system earns its place by working as usual on an ordinary day.

数据留在该留的地方Data stays where it belongs

敏感数据不必离开机构自己的环境。数据越敏感，保护措施越强。Sensitive data need not leave the organization's own environment. The more sensitive the data, the stronger the safeguards.

算力应当用得起Computing should be affordable

中小企业不该为用不上的算力付钱。先测量，再决定买、租还是共用。A smaller business should not pay for capacity it cannot use. Measure first, then decide whether to buy, rent or share.

先验证，再投入Validate, then invest

知道一个想法行不通，最便宜的时刻是在动手之前。The cheapest moment to learn that an idea does not work is before you build it.

人来做决定People make the decisions

AI 提出建议，人来判断。重要的输出在生效之前有人复核。AI proposes and people judge. Outputs that matter are checked by a person before they take effect.
