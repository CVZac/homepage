灵活、划算的算力方案，让中小企业也能用上 AI、放手创新。Flexible, cost-effective computing solutions that make AI adoption and innovation accessible to small and medium-sized businesses.

适合谁Who it is for

这条业务线适合想认真用 AI、但不想为此养一支技术团队的公司。This practice is for companies that want to use AI seriously without building a technical team to do it.

第一次上 AIAdopting AI for the first time
团队已经在零散地用，现在想把它正式用进业务。People already use it here and there. Now the business wants it properly in place.
没有专职运维No dedicated operations staff
没有人专门管服务器，系统需要足够简单，出了问题有人可以问。Nobody looks after servers full time. The setup has to be simple, with someone to ask when it breaks.
用量还说不准Usage is still uncertain
不想一次投入太多，希望先小规模用起来，再决定下一步。You would rather start small and decide the next step from what you learn than commit a large sum up front.

我们做什么What we do

按需选型Right-sized options

自有、租用还是共用，先看一段时间的真实用量再决定，不为用不上的算力付钱。Own, rent or share: we look at real usage over a period before deciding, so you do not pay for capacity you will not use.

硬件代采与部署Sourcing and setup

需要自有设备时，我们代为采购、安装和调试，交到你手里就能用。When owning makes sense, we source, install and configure the hardware and hand it over ready to use.

上手培训Getting the team started

教会团队日常怎么用、怎么问、哪些事不该交给它。We show the team how to use it day to day, how to ask, and what not to hand over to it.

随用量调整Adjusting with use

用得多了就加，用得少了就减，方案跟着业务走。More when you use more, less when you use less. The setup follows the business.

我们怎么做How we work on it

中小企业的预算经不起试错，所以我们从小处开始：先量一段时间的真实用量，再谈买什么。涉及客户或个人信息时，保护措施按负责任交付政策执行；合作中的做事方式见服务准则。A smaller business cannot afford expensive mistakes, so we start small: measure real usage for a while, then talk about what to buy. Where customer or personal information is involved, safeguards follow our responsible delivery policy; how we conduct an engagement is set out in the service charter.
