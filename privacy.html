01适用范围Scope

本政策适用于云想亿创集团的这个网站。我们为客户提供服务时如何处理数据，由双方签署的合同和服务准则约定，不在本政策范围内。This policy covers this website of CV-Etron Group. How we handle data when we provide services to clients is governed by the contract between us and by our service charter, and is outside this policy.

02本网站收集什么What this website collects

浏览本网站时，我们不收集你的个人信息。When you browse this website, we do not collect your personal information.

  • 本网站不使用 Cookie。This website uses no cookies.
  • 本网站不使用统计脚本或跟踪工具。This website uses no analytics scripts and no trackers.
  • 本网站不向第三方发起请求。页面使用的字体由本站提供。This website makes no requests to third parties. The fonts it uses are served from the site itself.
  • 你选择的语言保存在你自己的浏览器里（本地存储），不会发送给我们。清除浏览器数据即可删除。The language you choose is stored in your own browser (local storage) and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

和所有网站一样，托管本网站的服务器在响应访问时可能会生成访问日志，其中可能包含 IP 地址和访问时间。我们不使用这些日志识别访客。Like every website, the server that hosts this one may produce access logs when it answers a request, and these may include an IP address and the time of the visit. We do not use such logs to identify visitors.

03你主动发给我们的信息What you send us

联系页面上的表单不会把内容发送到任何服务器。它在你自己的邮件应用里生成一封邮件，是否发送由你决定。The form on the contact page sends nothing to any server. It composes an email in your own mail app, and whether to send it is up to you.

你给我们发邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和你写下的内容。我们用这些信息回复你，并完成你提出的事情，不作其他用途。When you email us, we receive your address and what you wrote. We use this to reply to you and to do what you asked, and for nothing else.

04我们如何保管How we keep it

来信保存在我们的工作邮箱里，只有需要处理它的团队成员可以看到。我们不出售、不出租，也不与无关的第三方分享你的信息。法律要求提供时除外。Messages are kept in our work mailbox, and only the team members who need to handle them can see them. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with unrelated third parties, except where the law requires it.

05你的权利Your rights

你可以要求我们说明保存了你的哪些信息，也可以要求更正或删除。写信到下面的联系地址即可。You may ask us what information of yours we hold, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to the contact address below.

06未成年人Children

本网站面向企业和成年的创业者，不面向未成年人。如果你认为有未成年人向我们发送了个人信息，请告诉我们，我们会删除。This website is intended for businesses and adult founders, not for children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, tell us and we will delete it.

07本政策的变更Changes to this policy

本政策有修改时，我们会更新本页顶部的生效日期。如果网站开始以新的方式处理信息，我们会先更新本政策。When this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If the website begins to handle information in a new way, we will update this policy first.

08联系方式Contact

关于本政策的任何问题，请写信到 sales@cv-etron.com。For any question about this policy, write to sales@cv-etron.com.
