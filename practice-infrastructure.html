企业级 AI 基础设施Enterprise-Grade AI Infrastructure

为模型部署、推理和业务应用提供可靠、可扩展的 AI 基础设施。Reliable, scalable AI infrastructure for model deployment, inference, and business applications.

适合谁Who it is for

这条业务线适合把 AI 当作正式系统来建设的机构。This practice is for organizations building AI as a permanent system.

数据不能外出Data that cannot leave
合同、病历、图纸、客户资料这类内容，需要留在机构自己的环境里处理。Contracts, medical records, drawings and customer files need to be processed inside the organization's own environment.
用量大而稳定Heavy, steady use
每天都有很多人在用，按量租用已经不划算，也不好预算。Many people use it every day, and paying by the hour has become both costly and hard to budget.
从试用转为正式Moving past the pilot
试用阶段的做法撑不住全员使用，需要按生产系统的标准重新搭建。What worked in a pilot will not carry the whole organization. It needs rebuilding to production standards.

我们做什么What we do

部署与推理Deployment and inference

按实际工作负载选定硬件与模型，在你的机房或专有环境里部署，把推理服务调到稳定可用。We choose hardware and models from the real workload, deploy them in your server room or private environment, and tune inference until it is stable.

接入业务系统Connecting business systems

把模型接进你已经在用的系统，让员工在原来的工作流程里用上它。We connect the models to the systems you already run, so people use them inside the work they already do.

知识库Knowledge bases

用机构自己的文档建立知识库，让回答有据可查，并且只对有权限的人可见。We build knowledge bases from your own documents, so answers can be traced to a source and are seen only by those allowed to see them.

运维与售后Operations and support

上线后持续看护：监控、更新、排障，以及用量变化时的调整。After launch we stay with the system: monitoring, updates, troubleshooting, and adjustments as usage changes.

我们怎么做How we work on it

先算工作负载，再选硬件。上线前，我们按数据的敏感程度确定部署等级和保护措施，用你的真实问题做测试，由你确认之后再上线。完整的做法写在服务准则和负责任交付政策里。We size the workload before we choose hardware. Before launch we set the deployment level and safeguards by how sensitive the data is, test with your real questions, and go live only once you have signed off. The full account is in our service charter and responsible delivery policy.
