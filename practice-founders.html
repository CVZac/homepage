适合谁Who it is for

这条业务线适合一个人，或者两三个人的事业。This practice is for a business of one, or of two or three.

想把专长做成产品的专业人士Professionals with expertise to package
你在一个领域做了很久，想把经验变成别人愿意付费的东西。You have worked in a field for a long time and want to turn that experience into something people will pay for.
独自创业的人Solo founders
没有团队，每一件事都要自己来，需要 AI 把一个人的时间放大。There is no team and everything falls to you. You need AI to stretch one person's time.
有主业、想先试一试的人People testing an idea alongside a job
还没有辞职，想用最小的投入先看看有没有人需要。You have not left your job, and you want to see whether anyone needs this at the smallest possible cost.

我们做什么What we do

从专长到产品From expertise to an offer

把你会的事拆开，找出其中可以重复交付、可以定价的部分。We break down what you know how to do and find the part that can be delivered repeatedly and priced.

AI 工具与算力AI tools and computing

按一个人的用量配置工具和算力，够用就好。Tools and computing sized for one person's use. Enough is enough.

验证与起步Validation and first customers

先验证有人愿意付费，再找到头几位客户。First confirm that someone will pay, then find the first few customers.

需要时对接资本Capital when it is time

不是每一门生意都需要融资。需要的时候，我们帮你做准备和对接。Not every business needs outside money. When yours does, we help you prepare and make introductions.

我们怎么做How we work on it

一个人的公司，每一步都要省。我们只建议当前阶段用得上的东西，并且先验证，再投入。合作中的做事方式见服务准则。In a company of one, every step has to be cheap. We recommend only what the current stage can use, and we validate before you invest. How we conduct an engagement is set out in the service charter.
