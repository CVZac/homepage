01概览Overview

上面四条是我们的优先顺序。它们冲突的时候，排在前面的优先。例如，照实说明一个系统还不能上线，比按期交付更重要。The four points above are our order of priority. When they conflict, the earlier one wins. Saying plainly that a system is not ready to go live, for example, matters more than delivering on schedule.

这份准则适用于我们全部五条业务线。This charter applies to all five of our practices.

02开始之前Before we start

  • 我们说明能做什么，也说明不能做什么。不适合我们的项目，我们会直说。We say what we can do and what we cannot. When a project is not right for us, we say so.
  • 范围、周期和交付物以书面形式约定。Scope, timeline and deliverables are agreed in writing.
  • 费用在开始工作之前以书面形式约定。约定之外的工作，先说明、经你同意，再进行。Fees are agreed in writing before work begins. Work beyond what was agreed is explained first and goes ahead only with your consent.

03数据与保密Data and confidentiality

  • 我们只索取工作所需的数据。We ask only for the data the work needs.
  • 客户的数据只用于客户自己的项目。Client data is used only for the client's own project.
  • 采用本地部署时，数据留在客户自己的环境里。With on-premises deployment, the data stays in the client's own environment.
  • 我们不使用客户的数据为其他任何人训练模型。We do not use client data to train models for anyone else.
  • 保密义务在合作结束之后继续有效。Confidentiality continues after the engagement ends.

部署时的具体保护措施，按负责任交付政策中的等级执行。The specific safeguards for a deployment follow the levels in our responsible delivery policy.

04交付Delivery

  • 我们以短周期推进，尽早交出可以使用的版本。We work in short cycles and hand over a usable version early.
  • 客户随时可以看到进展。出现延误或问题，我们主动说明。The client can see progress at any time. When there is a delay or a problem, we raise it ourselves.
  • 验收以事先写下的内容为准。Acceptance is judged against what was written down beforehand.
  • 经 AI 协助完成的工作成果，在交给客户之前由人复核。Work produced with the help of AI is reviewed by a person before it goes to the client.

05上线之后After launch

  • 我们交付文档并提供培训，让客户的团队能够自己使用和维护系统。We hand over documentation and provide training so that the client's team can use and maintain the system themselves.
  • 客户知道出了问题该联系谁。The client knows whom to contact when something goes wrong.
  • 持续支持的范围，在合同中写明。The scope of ongoing support is set out in the contract.

06与创业者合作Working with founders

  • 建议就是建议，决定由创业者自己作出。Advice is advice. The decisions are the founder's own.
  • 我们的服务不构成投资建议。Our services are not investment advice.
  • 我们不承诺任何融资结果。We do not promise any fundraising outcome.
  • 我们认为一个想法还没有准备好的时候，会直说，并说明理由。When we think an idea is not ready, we say so and give our reasons.

07我们不做的事What we will not do

  • 夸大可以达到的结果。Overstate the results that can be achieved.
  • 隐瞒已经发现的问题。Conceal a problem we have found.
  • 承接我们没有能力做好的工作。Take on work we are not equipped to do well.
  • 未经许可使用客户的名称或数据。Use a client's name or data without permission.
