启用集团名称：云想亿创集团Now operating as CV-Etron Group

即日起，我们以「云想亿创集团」和 CV-Etron Group 的名称对外。From today we operate under the names 云想亿创集团 and CV-Etron Group.

发布日期Published
2026年11月10日Nov 10, 2026
分类Category
公司Company
发布方Published by
云想亿创集团CV-Etron Group

名字由两部分组成。「云想」说的是点子在云上：好想法不该被困在某一台机器、某一家公司里。「亿创」说的是亿万人一起创造：AI 的意义，在于让更多人有能力把想法做出来。The Chinese name has two parts. 云想, "cloud-thought", is about ideas that live in the cloud: a good idea should not be trapped in one machine or one company. 亿创, "a hundred million creating", is about the many: the point of AI is that more people gain the means to build what they imagine.

点子在云上汇聚，未来由亿万人共创。Ideas gather in the cloud. The future is built by hundreds of millions of people.

我们的口号没有变：以人为核，智能环绕。技术围着人转，而不是相反。Our slogan has not changed: people at the core, intelligence in orbit. Technology revolves around people, not the reverse.

其他也没有变。联系邮箱仍是 sales@cv-etron.com，网站备案号仍是京ICP备2026070946号，五条业务线照常进行。Nothing else changes. The contact address is still sales@cv-etron.com, the ICP filing number is still 京ICP备2026070946号, and the five practices carry on as before.

关于我们是谁、相信什么，请看关于我们。For who we are and what we believe, see About us.
