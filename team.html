团队Team

我们是一支小而资深的团队：做过大规模互联网产品，现在用 AI 的方式做 AI。We are a small, senior team: people who built large internet products and now build AI the AI-native way.

三个特点，以及它们对客户意味着什么Three traits, and what each means for a client

资深互联网专家Veteran internet practitioners

团队成员来自互联网行业，做过许多人同时在用的产品。所以我们关心的不只是系统能不能跑通，还有它在很多人一起用的时候稳不稳。The team comes from the internet industry and has built products used by many people at once. So we care not only whether a system runs, but whether it holds when many people use it together.

AI NativeAI-native

我们自己的工作流程就建立在 AI 之上。给客户的建议，是我们每天都在用的做法，包括它不好用的地方。Our own workflows run on AI. What we recommend to clients is what we use every day, including the parts that do not work well.

敏捷开发迭代Short cycles, constant iteration

我们以短周期交付，尽早把可以用的版本交到使用者手里，再根据他们的反馈修改。客户不需要等到最后才看到东西。We deliver in short cycles, get a usable version to its users early, and revise on their feedback. A client does not wait until the end to see something.

我们怎样分工How we are organized

团队按五条业务线分工。每一次合作，我们这边都有一位负责人，客户随时可以找到他。The team is organized by the five practices. Every engagement has one owner on our side, whom the client can always reach.

  • 企业级 AI 基础设施Enterprise-Grade AI Infrastructure
  • 中小企业普惠算力Accessible Computing for SMBs
  • AI 商业验证AI-Powered Business Validation
  • 早期创业融资对接Venture Capital Access for Early-Stage Startups
  • 赋能独立创业者Empowering Independent Entrepreneurs

关于成员名单About names

我们不在网站上列出成员名单。合作开始时，你会认识与你共事的每一个人。We do not list team members on this site. When an engagement begins, you will meet everyone you work with.
