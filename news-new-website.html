云想亿创集团新官网上线CV-Etron Group launches its new website

今天，云想亿创集团的新官网正式上线。旧版只有一页，新版把内容分成四个部分，每一部分回答一个问题。CV-Etron Group's new website goes live today. The old site was a single page. The new one has four parts, and each part answers one question.

发布日期Published
2026年11月23日Nov 23, 2026
分类Category
公司Company
发布方Published by
云想亿创集团CV-Etron Group

新官网的四个部分是：The four parts are:

  • 研究：我们在一线项目里反复遇到的问题，以及我们对这些问题的想法。Research: the questions we keep meeting in the field, and what we think about them.
  • 观点：我们对 AI 应该怎样进入企业和创业的公开立场。Perspectives: our public positions on how AI should enter businesses and start-ups.
  • 承诺：服务准则、负责任交付政策、透明度说明，以及安全与合规。Commitments: the service charter, the responsible delivery policy, our transparency notes, and security and compliance.
  • 公司：我们是谁、团队如何组成、怎样加入。Company: who we are, how the team is built, and how to join.

五条业务线各有自己的页面，说明适合谁、我们做什么、怎么做。Each of the five practices has its own page, saying who it is for, what we do and how we do it.

这次改版先把结构和表达做清楚。价格与案例没有收录在这一版里。This release concentrates on structure and clarity. Pricing and case studies are not part of it.

网站本身也遵守我们写下的原则：不使用 Cookie 和统计脚本，字体由本站提供，联系表单不向任何服务器发送内容。细节见隐私政策。The site follows the principles it describes: no cookies and no analytics scripts, fonts served from the site itself, and a contact form that sends nothing to any server. The details are in the privacy policy.

有任何问题或建议，请写信到 sales@cv-etron.com。Questions and suggestions are welcome at sales@cv-etron.com.
