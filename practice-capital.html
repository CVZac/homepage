早期创业融资对接Venture Capital Access for Early-Stage Startups

为 A 轮前的创业团队对接投资人与创投资源，帮助做好融资准备、支持早期成长。Investor connections and venture resources for pre-Series A startups, supporting fundraising readiness and early-stage growth.

联系我们Contact us

适合谁Who it is for

这条业务线适合 A 轮之前的创业团队。This practice is for start-up teams before their Series A.

准备第一次机构融资Preparing a first institutional round
产品已经有了，接下来要面对专业投资人的提问。The product exists. Next come the questions of professional investors.
故事和数据还没理顺A story and numbers not yet in order
有早期用户，但讲不清楚问题有多大、钱怎么赚。There are early users, but the size of the problem and the way money is made are not yet clear in the telling.
不确定现在是不是时机Unsure whether now is the time
想先听一个不带立场的判断：现在融，还是再等一等。You want a view from someone with no stake in the answer: raise now, or wait.

我们做什么What we do

融资准备Fundraising readiness

把故事、证据、商业模型和公司文件整理到投资人看得懂、查得到的程度。We bring the story, the evidence, the business model and the company documents to the point where an investor can follow them and check them.

投资人对接Investor introductions

在阶段和方向都合适的情况下，把团队介绍给投资人。是否投资，由投资人自己判断。Where stage and focus match, we introduce the team to investors. Whether to invest is the investor's own decision.

早期成长支持Early-growth support

融资前后，用我们的 AI 与验证能力，帮团队把产品和业务往前推。Before and after a round, we use our AI and validation work to help the team move the product and the business forward.

我们怎么做How we work on it

我们先看准备程度，再谈对接。准备不足时我们会直说，并指出差在哪里。任何准备都不能保证融到钱，我们也不这样承诺。合作中的做事方式见服务准则。We look at readiness first and introductions second. When a team is not ready we say so, and we say what is missing. No amount of preparation guarantees funding, and we do not promise it. How we conduct an engagement is set out in the service charter.
